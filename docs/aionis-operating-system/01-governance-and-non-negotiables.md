# Governance and Non-Negotiables

## Authority order

1. Human-approved constitutional methodology and repository non-negotiables.
2. Approved feature, security, intelligence, and release decisions.
3. Versioned operational guidance and accepted answers.
4. Generated proposals and hypotheses.

Generated output never outranks evidence or approval. Global learning and either Dream loop may propose constitutional change but must never apply it automatically.

## Protected rules

- Preserve the calculation source and values unless formula approval and its audit workflow are explicit.
- Keep raw student information out of development telemetry and development memory.
- Keep private owner systems outside the public student application.
- Preserve tenant and user isolation, least privilege, provenance, version history, and rollback.
- Treat immutable evidence as append-only; correct derived conclusions by supersession, not evidence rewriting.
- Require the applicable Plan, Code, Release, or Dream Gate for meaningful architectural, behavioral, intelligence, security, or production changes.

## Classification language

- **Confirmed fact:** directly supported by adequate evidence.
- **Inferred pattern:** synthesis supported by evidence but not directly observed.
- **Active hypothesis:** plausible and unresolved; requires inquiry or more evidence.
- **Negative knowledge:** a rejected, disproved, unsafe, or superseded proposal retained to prevent repetition.
- **Confidence:** calibrated strength of support, never a substitute for provenance.
- **Staleness:** age or changed dependencies that may reduce usefulness.
- **Confidence decay:** a documented reduction caused by time, contradictory evidence, or dependency drift.
- **Supersession:** a new version replaces a prior conclusion while preserving history.
- **Rollback:** a reviewed restoration path to a known version.

Human approval must identify the artifact, version, scope, gate, approver, timestamp, and decision.
