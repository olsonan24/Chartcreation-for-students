# Aionis Intelligence Review

1. Apply `.agents/skills/aionis-ai-workflow/SKILL.md`; read `docs/aionis-operating-system/01-governance-and-non-negotiables.md`, `06-knowledge-and-memory-architecture.md`, `07-evidence-lineage-and-versioning.md`, and the relevant `09-human-pattern-dreaming.md` or `10-global-intelligence-learning.md` document in that directory.
2. Use `docs/aionis-operating-system/templates/intelligence-review.md` and inspect the exact generation manifest and evidence records.
3. Separate confirmed facts, inferred patterns, active hypotheses, and negative knowledge.
4. Verify source provenance, supporting and contradictory evidence, rule/modifier versions, confidence calibration/decay, conditional synthesis, and Inquiry behavior.
5. Confirm generated conclusions are not self-supporting evidence and private learning cannot leak across users or into global methodology.
6. Identify sensitivity, bias, language-tier, reproducibility, supersession, rollback, and constitutional impact.
7. Return approve, approve with conditions, or reject. Route methodology changes through the Dream Gate and release changes through the Release Gate.
